# Product glossary

## Shared knowledge concepts

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Official source | The authoritative system or document maintained by its owner. An index or a second-brain note may point to it, but does not replace it. |
| Derived context | An extracted, indexed, or summarized representation of one or more sources, retained to help an agent find and use relevant knowledge. It must keep provenance and must not be presented as the original source. |
| Provenance | Information that identifies where a statement came from, including a stable link to the original and, where available, its owner and last-modified time. |
| Freshness | An explicit indication of when source content was last checked or synchronized, and whether it is current, stale, or unknown. Freshness does not by itself prove accuracy. |
| Validation status | Whether a derived summary or interpretation has been reviewed by an accountable person. Unreviewed inferences must not be presented as verified facts. |
| Shared vocabulary | Centrally maintained terms, definitions, and tagging rules. Teams may propose additions; the central governance validates and maintains the shared reference. |

Product-specific terms belong with their owning product documentation. For example, CIQ defines **External Price Context**, **Price Trend**, and **Price index** in its [Strategy & Discovery page](https://decathlon.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/DI/pages/2555642327/CIQ+-+Strategy+Discovery); these are CIQ concepts, not universal OneSquad labels.
